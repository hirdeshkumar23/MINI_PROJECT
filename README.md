# MINI_PROJECT
Introduction – AI Fitness Coach →
Overview:
Many students and professionals struggle to maintain a healthy lifestyle due to lack of time,
motivation, and personalized guidance. Most fitness apps offer generic plans that don’t fit
individual needs.
Our Solution:

The AI Fitness Coach is an intelligent, personalized health platform that designs custom
workout routines, diet plans, and offers motivation via an AI chatbot.
Goal:

To help users achieve their fitness goals through personalized recommendations, progress
tracking, and real-time motivation — all in one app.
Problem:

• No personalized guidance
• Poor progress tracking
• Lack of motivation
• Time constraints
Value:

AI Fitness Coach provides tailored plans, real-time insights, and AI-driven support — a smart,
affordable fitness companion for everyone.
